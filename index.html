About

Created by two kids who love to build

BuildBot Kids was started in 2026 by two kids with a 3D printer and a lot of ideas. They design the prints, pick the colors and watch every piece come to life one layer at a time.

It's about more than toys. Every print is a small lesson in 3D printing, creativity, design and STEM, and proof that an idea on a screen can become something you can hold in your hand.
